Core Engine

ICT/SMC Signal Engine

A full ICT/SMC state-machine engine. Each trade is gated by H1 EMA bias alignment, then by an M15 liquidity sweep, market structure shift, and displacement candle. The setup quality is then scored on an 8-point confluence model and only setups scoring ≥4 are executed.

Each instrument runs only during its own optimal session: EUR/USD during London + NY open, NAS100 during the New York session, XAU/USD across the London/NY overlap. EUR/USD and NAS100 trade as contrarian fades of the SMC trap; XAU/USD trades the continuation.

Risk Management

Built to protect capital first

Position size is computed from each setup’s exact stop distance, not a fixed pip count, so risk per trade is always 1% regardless of volatility regime. Risk is automatically halved when an instrument enters drawdown, and additionally scaled by current ATR vs. its 80-bar median to dampen news-spike entries.

XAU/USD takes a 0.5R partial at +1R and trails the runner with an ATR-multiple stop. EUR/USD & NAS100 trade once and exit cleanly. A per-symbol kill switch pauses any instrument that hits –5% in a single window — the rest of the portfolio keeps trading.

Validation

Four-mode backtesting

Nothing is cherry-picked. The engine is sequential bar-by-bar with strict no-lookahead enforcement — H1 bias only reads fully-closed candles, and entries fire on the next M15 bar’s open with realistic spread + slippage applied to both entry and TP fills.

The same engine validates the strategy four ways: 70/30 out-of-sample split, calendar-walk over 15 random months across 2.5 years, 500-shuffle Monte Carlo, and a stacked-portfolio simulation. All three instruments come out positive across all four modes.
